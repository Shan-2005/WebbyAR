🖨️ Print Hiro Marker

Print this marker and place it flat on a surface. Point your phone camera at it to trigger the AR experience.

📄 Print on plain white paper — avoid glossy paper as it causes camera reflections.
📐 Keep the printed marker flat. Curved or wrinkled paper will confuse the tracker.
💡 Good lighting is key — avoid dark conditions or harsh direct light glare.
📏 Hold your phone 15–40 cm from the marker for best detection.
