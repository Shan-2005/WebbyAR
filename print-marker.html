🖨️ Print the Hiro Marker

This is the official AR.js Hiro marker — the exact pattern your phone's camera tracks. Print it, place it flat on a table, then open the AR app on your phone.

HIRO · AR.js Official Marker
📄 Print on plain white paper — glossy paper causes reflections that confuse tracking.
📐 Keep the marker flat on a hard surface. Curved or wrinkled paper won't track correctly.
💡 Make sure the area is well-lit — avoid shadows falling across the marker.
📏 Hold your phone 20–50 cm away from the marker and keep it steady for a moment.
🚫 Do not use a QR code as a marker — the app only recognises this specific Hiro pattern.
